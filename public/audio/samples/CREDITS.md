# Animal sound credits

All animal sounds in REPLICO are real recordings (not synthesized), sourced
from three places, each under a license permitting reuse. Files were
auto-trimmed to their loudest ~2.4s and loudness-normalized from the
originals; source links below point at the untouched originals.
